Our Heritage

Born in Panruti,
Tamil Nadu's Cashew Heart

Panruti, located in Cuddalore district, is the undisputed cashew capital of India. For over three decades, our farmers have cultivated world-class cashews in the region's unique red laterite soil and tropical climate.

Our platform was built to eliminate exploitative middlemen and ensure that farmers receive fair prices while buyers get the freshest, most genuine product directly from the source.
